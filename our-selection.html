Certified Seat™

What It Takes to Get Certified

Before any seat above earns a spot in the lineup, it must pass the full Certified Seat™ review:

  • The Sit Test — sat on, by a professional, for a length of time we consider proprietary.
  • The Stand-Up Test — can you get back up without assistance? (Bonus points if you don't want to.)
  • The Nap Clause — falling asleep mid-review disqualifies the reviewer, not the seat.
  • The Best Seat in the House Standard — wherever it's placed, it must become the best seat in that house.

Seats that pass all four earn the Certified Seat™ seal. Seats that don't are politely asked to try harder.
