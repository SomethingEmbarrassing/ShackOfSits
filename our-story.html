Our Story

A Legacy of Sitting

In the Beginning, There Was a Name

Every great institution begins with a founding document. Ours began with a founder's shaky handwriting, a squinting early investor, and a boardroom that read the name aloud before anyone had the courage to ask for clarification. We heard what you heard. We chose not to correct the record. We leaned in.

A Mission, Stated Plainly

Shack of Sits exists to answer a question the seating industry has spent decades avoiding: what if a place to sit... was also a place to sit? We believe in synergy. We believe in core competencies. Our core competency is seats.

Our Values

  • Integrity: Every seat is exactly as comfortable as we say it is.
  • Innovation: We didn't invent sitting. We just took it seriously.
  • Community: A rising tide lifts all seats.

Looking Ahead

As we continue to scale our seat-forward vision into new markets, one thing remains unchanged: somewhere, right now, someone is sitting down. We'd like that to be on one of ours.
