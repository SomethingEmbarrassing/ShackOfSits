This form uses Web3Forms — a free relay service that emails a submission straight to your inbox without your address ever appearing in the page source. No password or account needed, just a free access key:
  1. Go to web3forms.com and enter the email you want submissions sent to.
  2. You'll get a confirmation email with an Access Key.
  3. Paste that key into the hidden access_key field near the top of this file, replacing REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_WEB3FORMS_ACCESS_KEY.
That's it — no server, no exposed email, and it's free for reasonable volume. Once GoDaddy has a real site built (right now shackofsits.com is still just a "coming soon" page), this can also be swapped for GoDaddy's own built-in contact form block if that's easier.
